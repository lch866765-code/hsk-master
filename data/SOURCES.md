# Vocabulary Sources

## HSK 3.0 (2021) Word Lists

The vocabulary in `hsk3.json` (level 3) and `hsk4.json` (level 4) is based on the
**HSK 3.0 Standard Vocabulary List** published by the Chinese International Education Foundation
(汉考国际 / Hanban) in 2021.

- Official reference: [HSK 3.0 标准课程词汇大纲](https://www.chinesetest.cn/gosign.do)
- Open-source reference lists: [ivankra/hsk](https://github.com/ivankra/hsk) (MIT licence)

Korean meanings (`meaning_ko`) and example translations (`example_ko`) are original
translations authored for this project.
